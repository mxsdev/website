Terence Tao recently proposed this new era to be the [_"Copernican Revolution in intelligence"_](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.26524). Apparently, intelligence was not as distinctively human or supreme as we once thought. How astonished I was to hear this! I had read the same thing in Nietzsche, writing over a century ago:

> _In some remote corner of the universe, poured out and glittering in countless solar systems, there was once a star on which clever beasts invented knowing. That was the most arrogant and mendacious minute of 'world history,' but nevertheless, it was only a minute._

Nietzsche understood that we are far, far more than _intellects_ — and in so many of our activities, intellect plays a surprisingly small part. A striking proposition: so we humans are _displaced_ — what if now as _less_ impoverished?

Software engineering is now an arbitrage — most senior "humans in the loop" are embarrassingly less valuable than their salaries suggest. It is the worst time possible to optimize for compensation and titles, because we have no clue what they will be in the next few years. If our engineers want a new _task_, it should be this: to use this reprieve to find a new voice compatible with this new future.

What such new voices are available? The pro-AI voices are quite right that the anti-AI crowd are largely dissimulating their fear; but perhaps it _takes one to know one_. Is impetuously driving to rewrite everything in uncertain and untested technology any less driven by…_fear_? And fear of the same thing! What is certain: grand outcomes will precipitate no less from grand _courage_ in this new era than from the old one; and grand courage has no less its characteristic symptoms of _equanimity_, a scarce commodity today.

Here is our engineers' latest path: _taste_! Value is now in our _craftsmanship_! [Taste is All That's Left](https://notashelf.dev/posts/taste-is-all-thats-left)! Apparently, _taste_ is our ability to say "no" to agents, to stay headstrong in our _care_. My friends, as romantic a concept as this is - do you really believe yourselves? Is this deliberative process not something agents have shown time and time again to excel at? Are you really ready to argue that our very _taste_ is not being fed to the machines, too? These _software sommeliers_, however exquisite, stand downstream of an already-existing product, passively ranking what they are handed; and perhaps it is this _passivity_ they are really defending, with their illustrious _"taste"_.

What then, if not _taste_, are humans left with? It is none other than _creation_. You demand a _definition_ of creation? Do you not see how a definition would foreclose the very novelty _essential to_ creation? As long as we begin with _intellect_, as long as we _start_ with the agent, as long as we treat "AI" as a product — we have already dived head-first into the river of un-creation. We are then yielding any possible advantage a human could have _even in principle_. Say "no" to an amalgamated average, no matter how vigorously, you _do not escape that average_. The only winning move is not to play; not to play, but to invent a new play! To become the artist! To create not systems but _styles_!

Could there be style in _types and contracts_? This one will produce ingenious _invariants_ that solve these problems of security and determinism, as poets affect solemnity through the austerity of their prose. An artist of elegant constraints!

Could there be style in the _orchestration of agents_? It is the style of a great rhetorician, that can mediate and break up any argument, without either side feeling as though they _lost_. Our new politicians, our new _engineers of deliberation_.

Could there be style in _representing our physical world_? They say art imitates nature after all! And is one's _purpose_ not inseparable from one's _style_? "Context preparation is critical" — is this not an engineer's way of saying: it is beneficial to have more styles than realism! Our new stylists, our _cartographers_.

Could there be style in _distillation of business knowledge_? "Code is dead" misunderstands that it is the _web of human concerns the code represents_, and not the text on the screen, from which value is born. But this is a web! And a web is an _organic_ mess — and need I remind you that not even our greatest robots can _unwind knots_? I welcome our new knitters, our new embroiderers…

Oh, look around! Are there not so many new portraits ready to be fashioned? But so few _painters_. Perhaps the best portrait I can provide is that of the engineer that _paints their own portrait_ — with enough of these, we may one day look forward to a new beautiful _landscape_. And you're afraid of this? To create one's own style is thoroughly _unromantic_, and that is why we are so afraid.
